About

Ferry carries a payment across chains to a fixed destination.

Merchants want one asset at one address. Buyers hold whatever they hold, on whichever chain. Ferry connects the two with an exact-amount checkout built on NEAR Intents.

Why it exists

NEAR co-founder Illia Polosukhin suggested a "universal checkout widget" during the NEAR Hackathon (November 2026): a checkout button any merchant can add, so buyers can pay through Intents from the wallet and chain they already have. Ferry is our take on that idea, built for the hackathon.

What it is today

  • Hosted checkout links and an embeddable button for products and one-off invoices.
  • Exact-output quotes from the public NEAR Intents 1Click API. The destination amount is conditional on an eligible execution. amountIn is a buffered suggestion. minAmountIn is the minimum.
  • A reconciliation inbox: paid, processing, and needs attention, with stored quote evidence, a timeline, freshness, unknowns, resolution notes, and a CSV export.
  • Payment status checked on the server, with a strict match before any order is marked paid. A seen deposit is not settled. A provider refund is not a merchant refund.
  • Optional NEAR AI text in two places. A setup assistant drafts storefront copy for approval. An order explanation describes evidence Ferry already stored and drafts a review step. Neither one can change a price, a payout, or a payment. A historical copy draft was checked live on 2026-11-08. That does not mean the hosted key works now. Health stays unverified until a real response succeeds, and a rejected key stays auth_failed.

What it is not

Ferry is not a budget or spending assistant. It does not decide what a buyer can afford, and it does not move funds. Simulated cases on the dashboard and the sample store are marked SIMULATED. They are not live settled volume.

Honest limits

  • NEAR Intents has no testnet, so every non-dry quote is a real mainnet deposit address. No end-to-end mainnet payment has been verified for Ferry. Do not read the inbox counts as settled volume unless the provider evidence is there.
  • The quote deadline is when the deposit address can become inactive and funds may be lost. Ferry does not promise a late refund.
  • Supported assets change. Ferry reads the live 1Click list and claims nothing beyond it.
  • Payout addresses are limited to NEAR, EVM and Solana chains, because Ferry can only validate those formats.
  • Outbound merchant webhooks aren't built yet; see the docs.
  • This repository is independent of Tripwire, a separate codebase by the same author in the Memecoins With Mechanics track. The two projects share no code.

Built with

NEAR Intents 1Click API, NEAR AI Cloud, and Node.js with its built-in node:sqlite locally or Postgres (Neon) in production. The only runtime dependencies are pg and qrcode. MIT licensed.
