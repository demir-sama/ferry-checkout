Checkout and reconciliation

One destination. Their chain. A record you can check.

Ferry is a merchant checkout for NEAR Intents. You set the destination asset, the amount in base units, and the address. The buyer pays from a supported asset on its own chain. An exact-output quote asks for that destination amount only if an eligible swap completes. It is not fiat, and it is not a promise before the deposit.

What the merchant can know

PaidA stored quote reported SUCCESS and matched this order: address, memo, signature, asset, recipient, and amount. Anything else stays out of this lane.
ProcessingThe provider has a live deposit, a seen transaction, or a swap still executing. KNOWN_DEPOSIT_TX is not settlement.
Needs attentionPartial deposits, mismatched proofs, provider refunds, a second settlement, a failed refresh, or a deadline that has passed.

Open checkouts with no deposit in flight sit in their own lane. Simulated demonstrations are labelled SIMULATED and are not added to these counts. No mainnet settlement has been verified for this product.

How a payment moves

1 · OrderFerry fixes the item and the destination amount on the server. Those fields are not edited later, and the model cannot edit them.
2 · QuoteThe buyer picks a supported asset. Ferry stores an EXACT_OUTPUT quote, including amountIn, minAmountIn, the deposit address, any memo, and the signature.
3 · DepositThe buyer is shown the buffered suggestion and the minimum. A deposit after the quote deadline can put funds at risk. Ferry does not promise a late refund.
4 · EvidenceFerry reads provider status by deposit address and memo. Paid is only a matched SUCCESS. The inbox keeps the unknowns, the time of the last read, and your notes.

Rules first

Every order carries a deterministic diagnosis: what is known, what the provider left out, and which comparison used minAmountIn. The label says it is rules, not a model.

A narrative with no authority

NEAR AI can describe that evidence and draft a next step for you to review. It cannot change a price, a payout, or a payment, and it cannot send a refund or a message. If the key is missing or rejected, the inbox still works and says so.
