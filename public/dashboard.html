NEAR AI setup assistant

Describe your store. NEAR AI Cloud drafts a tagline, About text, button label, accent colour, product copy, FAQ and suggested payment assets into a strict schema. Ferry checks the draft and shows you a diff. Nothing is applied until you approve it.

The AI never sees or sets your payout address, prices, quotes or payment status.
